STAY SAFE
Trick or treating safety, the 30 second version
Kids go in a group with a grown up. Torch or glow sticks, stick to footpaths and lit houses only, look both ways before every driveway, and check treats before eating. Houses with a light on are saying yes, dark houses are saying no.
